PRIVACY

Your data

How to remove progress or contact the studio.

On your device

On Android, open Settings → Apps → game → Storage → Clear data. This deletes local progress and preferences. On PC, files vary by game; support can help you find them. Also manage backups and Steam Cloud copies, which may restore your data.

In studio support

To request access, correction or deletion of information sent to support, email leandrorogalabritezltda@gmail.com. Include the game, platform and context of your request. We may need to verify your identity. Do not send passwords or documents unless asked through an appropriate process.

Messages are kept as long as needed to handle and follow up on your request, then deleted or anonymized, unless retention is needed for legal obligations, security or legal claims.

In other services

There is no studio player account to close. Advertising data and Google, Steam or Unity account data are managed by those providers. Deleting a game or asking the studio for deletion does not automatically remove their records. Our Privacy Policy links to those services and explains your choices.

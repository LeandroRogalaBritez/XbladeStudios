Privacy Policy

Your data, explained in plain language.

Responsible developer: Xblade Studios — Leandro Rogala Britez LtdaLast updated: October 15, 2026Contact: leandrorogalabritezltda@gmail.com

This policy covers this website and Xblade Studios games that link to it. Our Android games are free, supported by Google AdMob ads, and currently have no in-app purchases. On PC, platform features are provided through Steam.

1. Game data

Progress, scores and preferences are saved on your device so you can continue playing. We do not require registration or operate our own player account server. Gameplay does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or precise GPS location.

2. Ads on Android

We use Google AdMob, with no other mediation networks configured by the studio. Depending on the game, ads appear between rounds or as optional ads that offer a reward.

Google collects and shares IP addresses (which may indicate an approximate region), device and app identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information. This data is used to serve and measure ads, analyze their operation and prevent fraud. The UMP consent service records and applies your choices. See Google's Privacy Policy and how data is used in partner apps.

Where applicable, you can review consent under Ad privacy in the game options. You can also manage your advertising identifier in Android settings. Non-personalized ads may still use data for delivery, measurement and security.

3. Steam and game technology

In Steam versions, accounts, downloads and features such as achievements, leaderboards and Steam Cloud are managed through the platform, where available. The game may access your Steam identifier and the data needed for those features. Leaderboards and profiles may be visible according to your Steam settings. Payments and refunds are handled by the store; we do not receive your full card details. See Valve's Privacy Policy. PC versions do not use AdMob.

In versions built with Unity, the game technology may also process IP addresses, identifiers, device information and diagnostics for operation, technical analysis and service improvement, depending on configuration. See Unity's Privacy Policy. Our catalog lists the services used by each version.

4. Website and contact

This website does not sell products or create accounts, and its code does not use analytics, ads or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and technical logs to host and protect the pages, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

If you contact support, we receive your email address, the name you provide and your message to respond to your request. Our email uses Google's Gmail service. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains while the game's files are kept. On Android, you can remove it through Settings → Apps → game → Storage → Clear data; this erases local progress. Backups and Steam Cloud copies must be managed through the relevant service.

Support messages are kept as long as needed to handle and follow up on your request, then deleted or anonymized, unless retention is needed for legal obligations, security or legal claims. To request access, correction or deletion of data we control, email leandrorogalabritezltda@gmail.com. There is no studio player account to close.

Data controlled by Google, Valve, Unity and GitHub follows their retention criteria and request channels. Deleting the game does not automatically remove their records. See our data management guidance.

6. Sharing and security

The providers above receive the data needed for the services described and may process it in other countries with their service providers. We may comply with valid legal obligations. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Protection of local files depends on the game and device; no system is completely secure.

7. Audience and updates

Our games are not specifically directed at children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent data to the studio, please contact us.

We will update this page when our practices change, with the revision date at the top. Privacy questions? Contact leandrorogalabritezltda@gmail.com.

What to include in your message

  1. Game title: tell us which Xblade Studios game you need help with.
  2. Platform and version: tell us whether you play on PC or Android, the store and the game version. On mobile, include your device model and OS version.
  3. What happened: describe the issue, what you expected and the steps to reproduce it.
  4. Screenshots and error messages: include them if helpful. Remove personal information before sending.

Do not send passwords, card details or authentication codes.

My game is crashing. Where should I start?

Restart the game, check for updates and note any error message. On Steam, you can also verify file integrity under Properties → Installed Files. If the problem persists, email us the details.

I have a question about a Steam purchase.

For Steam payments and refund requests, visit Steam Support. For gameplay or technical issues, contact the studio.

I have a question about my data.

See our Privacy Policy or the data requests page. Include the game, platform and context of your request.
